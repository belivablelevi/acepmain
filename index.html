Get outside. Have fun. Help the planet.

ACEP is a simple, family-friendly way to explore nature, learn about the environment, and earn points together — no screens required for the mission, just your curiosity and a photo when you’re done.

See challenges Create a free account

How it works

1. Sign up with a parent and pick a challenge.
2. Head outside and complete the mission in real life.
3. Upload a photo, earn points, and climb the leaderboard.

Why families like it

Short, clear activities. Kid-friendly language. Badges from 🌱 Beginner up to 🏆 Legend. It’s about fresh air, teamwork, and caring for Canada’s environment — not homework or volunteer hours.
