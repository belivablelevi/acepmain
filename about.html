About ACEP

We help families turn outdoor time into playful environmental action.

Our mission

The Alliance for Canadian Environmental Protection (ACEP) celebrates small, real-world steps that add up: picking up litter, saving water, spotting wildlife, and learning how pollution affects our communities.

Missions are written for kids roughly 8+ with grown-ups nearby when tasks need extra safety. Everything stays light, encouraging, and focused on time outside — not screens, not school credit, just curiosity and care.

What you’ll find

  • Ten clear challenges with points and difficulty labels
  • Photo uploads so kids can show what they did
  • Points, badges (Beginner → Legend), and a friendly leaderboard
  • A dashboard to see your progress and past photos

Safety first

Some challenges ask for a trusted adult when you’re near roads, water, or anything unfamiliar. If something feels unsafe, skip it and tell a grown-up.
